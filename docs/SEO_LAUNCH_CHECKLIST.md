# Search visibility launch checklist

The site now provides crawlable product URLs, a sitemap, canonical URLs, local-business markup, product offers, breadcrumbs, and FAQ markup. Complete these operational steps after every production deployment.

## One-time setup

1. Create or claim the **House of Nuts Proddatur** Google Business Profile. Use the exact website address, phone number, address, opening hours, and primary category shown on the website. Verify ownership.
2. Verify `https://houseofnuts.store/` in Google Search Console and submit `https://houseofnuts.store/sitemap.xml`.
3. Register the store with Google Merchant Center, opt into free product listings, and provide a compliant product feed. Product price, stock, title, image, and landing-page URL must match the site.
4. Add the verified Google Business Profile and social links to the same business identity. Do not create duplicate Business Profile listings.

## Weekly operating routine

1. Keep product titles, prices, availability, photos, and descriptions accurate in **Admin → Inventory**. Publish each batch once finished.
2. Ask genuine, completed-purchase customers for an honest Google review. Never offer incentives, write reviews for customers, or post fake reviews.
3. Publish useful local or product-led content: how to choose almonds, storing dry fruits in Andhra Pradesh weather, Diwali gifting, walnut quality grades, or recipes. Link to relevant shop categories from every article.
4. In Search Console, check Pages (indexing), Core Web Vitals, Product snippets, and search queries. Fix errors before adding more pages.

## Before publishing a promotion or new product

- Use a distinct product name and SKU; do not create a second record for the same item and pack sizes.
- Upload a real product image with a descriptive product name, not an unrelated generic image.
- Confirm the price, MRP, and stock are correct, then publish from Inventory.
- Check the public product URL on a phone and share it to WhatsApp/Instagram to confirm its title, image, and price are correct.

## Expectations

SEO makes the website easier to discover; it cannot guarantee a particular ranking. Local prominence also depends on accurate Business Profile data, proximity, review quality, competitor activity, reputable local mentions, and Google’s own ranking systems. Give indexing and ranking changes time to take effect after publishing.
